## Kunz 2025 Fact Check Details

Primary sources: PubMed PMID `40816265`; PMC full text `PMC12360486`.

- Correct title: `Inner speech in motor cortex and implications for speech neuroprostheses`
- Correct DOI: `10.1016/j.cell.2025.06.015`
- Journal/date: Cell, epub 2025-08-14, issue 2025-08-21
- Overall neural representation analysis: four BrainGate2 participants with motor cortex microelectrode arrays.
- Real-time self-paced inner-speech sentence decoding: three dysarthric participants.
- 50-word vocabulary WER: 24%, 14%, and 33% for T12, T15, and T16.
- 125,000-word vocabulary WER: 26% to 54% for T15 and T16.
- The paper itself notes prior ECoG and SMG intracortical inner-speech decoding work, so the milestone claim should not be "first imagined speech decoding" broadly. The safer contribution is: motor-cortex intracortical representation of inner speech plus real-time self-paced large-vocabulary inner-speech BCI proof of concept.
